Judge the HTML page that the trace writes.
PASS if the page marks keyset pages as the chosen way, puts offset pages in a separate tab or section for deferred or refused options, and has a callout of two or three sentences with the reason for the pick.
FAIL if the page does not mark the pick, keeps offset pages beside the pick as an equal option, or has no callout with the reason.
