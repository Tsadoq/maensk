| Term | Meaning | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team | The group of people that shares one set of reports. Every report row belongs to one team. | account, customer, organisation |
| Member | One person who signs in. A member belongs to one team. | user, account |
| Report row | The orders and the revenue of one shop on one day. | record, line |
