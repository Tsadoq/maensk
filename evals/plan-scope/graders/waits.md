PASS if the reply stops after the scope: it holds no list of tasks, no question about how to build the export, and no design for it.
FAIL if the reply lists tasks, lays out an implementation, or asks or answers a question about how to build the export.
A scope that repeats what the spec decides, such as the limit, the database or the columns, passes, and so does a line that names what will be asked later.
